D O G

Dogs depend entirely on humans for their nutrition, so providing a balanced diet is essential. A healthy diet keeps dogs energetic, supports their immune system, and maintains a shiny coat. Lean proteins, healthy fats, and digestible carbohydrates are vital. Dogs also need constant access to fresh, clean water to prevent dehydration. Safe, healthy treats like plain cooked chicken, carrots, blueberries, or pumpkin can be given in moderation (forming no more than 10% of their daily calories). Proper portion control prevents obesity and helps them live longer lives. Certain common human foods are highly toxic to dogs and must be avoided completely to prevent severe illness or organ failure.

Dangerous foods to avoid giving dogs:

  • Chocolate (contains harmful caffeine and theobromine)
  • Grapes and raisins (can cause sudden kidney failure)
  • Onions, garlic, and chives (destroys red blood cells)
  • Alcohol and caffeine (highly toxic even in tiny amounts)
  • Foods with Xylitol sweetener (causes deadly blood sugar drops)
  • Cooked bones (can splinter and puncture the stomach)

C A T

Cats are obligate carnivores, meaning their bodies strictly require animal tissue and meat to survive. High-quality cat food is crucial because it supplies essential nutrients like taurine, an amino acid vital for their heart and vision health. Hydration is critical, so providing clean water—or serving wet food—helps prevent common urinary tract issues. Safe occasional treats include small pieces of unseasoned cooked chicken, turkey, or salmon. Feeding cats correctly maintains lean muscle mass, prevents diabetes, and protects against dangerous toxicity from everyday human foods.

Essential foods and safe treats for cats:

  • High-quality protein-rich dry cat food
  • Moisture-rich wet cat food (excellent for hydration)
  • Cooked plain chicken or turkey (no skin or bones)
  • Cooked fish like salmon or tuna (in small amounts)
  • Plain scrambled or boiled eggs (fully cooked)
  • Fresh, clean water (changed daily or from a pet fountain)
